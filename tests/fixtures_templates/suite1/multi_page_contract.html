Master Services Agreement: Terms & Recitals

This Enterprise Master Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into on this 6th day of November, 2026, by and between PaperPilot Technologies Inc., a Delaware corporation having its principal place of business at 100 Technology Plaza, San Francisco, CA (“Company”), and Global Global Enterprises LLC, a California corporation (“Client”).

Recitals & Intent

WHEREAS, Company possesses proprietary expertise in document processing, high-assurance digital impositions, cryptographic signatures, and automation runtimes; and WHEREAS, Client desires to retain Company to perform enterprise document pipeline infrastructure services as described herein;

Core Instrument Notice: This document consists of eight (8) definitive pages constituting the entire understanding between the parties.

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 “Deliverables” means all computer code, documentation, specifications, transformation pipelines, and reports generated under any applicable Statement of Work.
1.2 “Proprietary Materials” means pre-existing intellectual property, algorithms, fonts, and tooling owned or licensed by Company.

Article 1: Scope of Services & Technical Milestones

Company agrees to furnish engineering services, cloud and desktop orchestration runtime deployments, and document pipeline optimizations in accordance with the schedule and criteria set forth in applicable Statements of Work.

1.1 Operational Obligations

  1. Company shall maintain 99.99% uptime availability for automated cloud transform cluster endpoints.
  2. All document manipulation kernels shall comply with strict memory safety guarantees and zero external network leaks.
  3. Client shall be granted access to automated test harnesses validating compliance across all 90 enterprise tool operations.

1.2 Change Management Procedure

Any modification to the functional requirements or architectural parameters of the services shall be submitted in writing via a formal Change Request Order. Company shall provide an impact assessment within five (5) business days detailing cost and schedule adjustments.

Service Level Commitment: Critical bugs affecting document integrity shall be mitigated within four (4) hours of receipt.

Article 2: Fees, Invoicing & Financial Settlement

Client shall compensate Company in accordance with the fee schedule specified in each executed Statement of Work. Unless otherwise specified, all amounts are quoted in United States Dollars (USD).

2.1 Invoicing and Payment Terms

2.1.1 Monthly Recurring Charges: Invoiced thirty (30) days in advance of the service commencement cycle.
2.1.2 Net 30 Terms: All undisputed invoices shall be paid in full within thirty (30) days from invoice delivery date.
2.1.3 Late Payment Surcharge: Delinquent balances shall accrue interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permissible under applicable law.

2.2 Taxes and Withholdings

All fees are exclusive of applicable federal, state, or municipal sales, use, value-added, or withholding taxes, which shall be the sole responsibility of Client.

Article 3: Intellectual Property & Work Product Ownership

The parties recognize the distinction between pre-existing proprietary background technology and custom project deliverables created under this Agreement.

3.1 Client Ownership of Work Product

Upon Company’s receipt of full payment for services rendered, Company assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in and to custom software modules and configuration artifacts created specifically for Client.

3.2 Reservation of Company Rights

Company retains all rights, title, and interest in and to the PaperPilot Core Engine, foundational PDF manipulation kernels, pre-trained machine learning weights, and general reusable software utilities.

License Grant: Company grants Client a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive license to use embedded Company background technology solely in connection with the Deliverables.

Article 4: Confidentiality & Data Protection Standards

During the term of this Agreement, either party may disclose proprietary or confidential technical, financial, or business information to the other party.

4.1 Confidentiality Obligations

  1. The receiving party agrees to protect the disclosing party’s Confidential Information with the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential data, but in no event less than reasonable care.
  2. Confidential Information shall not be disclosed to any third party without prior written consent, except to employees and contractors with a need to know.
  3. Exclusions: Information that is publicly known, already known without restriction, independently developed, or rightfully obtained from third parties.

4.2 Data Protection & Security Compliance

Company agrees to adhere to SOC 2 Type II controls, GDPR privacy regulations, and HIPAA data handling practices whenever processing customer data payloads.

Article 5: Warranties, Representations & Indemnification

Each party represents and warrants that it has the legal power and authority to enter into and perform its obligations under this Agreement.

5.1 Performance Warranty

Company warrants that the services will be performed in a professional, workmanlike manner conforming to prevailing industry standards. Client must notify Company of any warranty non-conformity within ninety (90) days of delivery.

5.2 Mutual Indemnification

5.2.1 Company Indemnity: Company shall defend and indemnify Client against third-party claims that the Deliverables infringe valid patents or copyrights.
5.2.2 Client Indemnity: Client shall defend and indemnify Company against claims arising from materials or specifications provided by Client.
Limitation of Liability: Total aggregate liability of either party shall be capped at the total fees paid under the applicable SOW in the preceding twelve months.

Article 6: Term, Termination & Dispute Resolution

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and shall continue in effect for an initial period of three (3) years, renewing automatically for successive one-year terms unless either party provides sixty (60) days prior written termination notice.

6.1 Termination for Cause

Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches any provision and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) calendar days of receiving written notice specifying the breach.

6.2 Effects of Termination

Upon termination, Client shall pay Company for all work performed up to the termination date. Each party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information belonging to the other party.

Survival: Articles 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7 shall survive any expiration or termination of this Agreement.
