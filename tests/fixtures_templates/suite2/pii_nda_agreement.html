1. PURPOSE AND INTENT

The Disclosing Party and the Receiving Party wish to explore a potential strategic business acquisition, capital restructuring, and joint software engineering partnership. In connection with discussions concerning this opportunity, each party may disclose to the other certain proprietary and confidential information relating to its customer records, cryptographic keys, algorithm architectures, financial ledger balances, and corporate strategy.

2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" refers to any proprietary information, technical data, trade secrets, know-how, software source code, specifications, customer PII (including social security numbers, emails, and financial accounts), and business models disclosed by one party to the other, whether orally, in writing, electronically, or by inspection of tangible objects.

3. NON-DISCLOSURE OBLIGATIONS & SECURITY CONTROLS

Each party agrees to maintain all Confidential Information in strict confidence and to use at least the same degree of care as it uses for its own confidential information of like importance, but in no event less than a reasonable standard of enterprise cryptographic and administrative security. Receiving Party shall not disclose, duplicate, reverse engineer, or transmit any Confidential Information to third parties without prior written authorization.

4. FINANCIAL CONSIDERATION & LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

The parties acknowledge that unauthorized dissemination of Confidential Information will cause irreparable injury for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate.

5. TERM AND TERMINATION

The obligations of confidentiality under this Agreement shall survive for a period of thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date, or in perpetuity with respect to any trade secrets and personally identifiable customer information under applicable data protection laws (including GDPR and CCPA).

6. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION OF MATERIALS

Upon written notice from the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly destroy or return all documents, materials, prototypes, notes, and digital recordings containing or reflecting Confidential Information, providing a sworn certification of destruction executed by an officer within ten (10) business days.
