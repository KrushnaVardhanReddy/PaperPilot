## Required Test Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Implicit Active Document Binding & In-Place Execution
1. Navigate to `/` and load `tests/e2e_fixtures/multi_page.pdf` via `input[type="file"]`.
2. Click `#right-panel-tab-chat` to open the AI Chat tab.
3. Enter natural language query: `"Rotate 90 degrees clockwise"`.
4. Verify the assistant responds with an `EditableActionCard` (`.plan-card`):
   - Header contains `Rotate Operation`.
   - `All Pages` is selected by default.
   - Angle `90°` button is `.active`.
5. Click `button.execute-btn` (`⚡ Execute Action`).
6. Verify `.result-card` appears with `.result-badge` displaying `✅ Success`.
7. Verify the output file referenced in `.result-path-box .path-value` exists on disk and is non-empty.

### Scenario 2: Parameter Customization in Action Blueprint
1. In the chat, type: `"Rotate 180 degrees"`.
2. In the resulting `.plan-card`:
   - Click `Custom Pages` segmented button.
   - Fill `#custom-pages-input` with `'1'`.
   - Toggle Angle button to `270°`.
3. Click `⚡ Execute Action`.
4. Verify execution succeeds and `.result-card` is rendered with output path.

### Scenario 3: Cheat Sheet Integration & One-Click Prompt Insertion
1. Click `.examples-btn` (`💡 Examples`).
2. Verify `.cheatsheet-drawer` is visible.
3. Filter search with `"compress"`.
4. Click `[Try Prompt ↗]` on the Compress action.
5. Verify `.input-area input` is populated with the prompt text and the cheatsheet closes.
6. Press Enter to submit the prompt.
7. Verify `.plan-card` renders with `Compress Operation`.
8. Click `⚡ Execute Action` and verify `.result-card` confirms completion.
