OBJECTIVE:
Refactor the PaperPilot desktop application to an integrated, modern 3-pane workspace on desktop (>1024px) with seamless, fluid mobile & tablet adaptations (<768px):
1. Left Pane (Desktop 240px) / Slide-over Drawer (Mobile): Library & Automations
   - Recent files with category badges and quick actions
   - Smart Categories generated by on-device SLM (Contracts, Invoices, Scans, "To Review" amber badge)
   - Watched Folders with pulsing green active badge and popover controls
   - Shared With You files list with avatar pills
2. Center Canvas: Focused Document Viewer
   - Warm-white canvas (#F9F7F5) with soft ambient drop-shadow
   - Desktop Floating Pill Toolbar (frosted glass, backdrop-filter: blur(12px)): Page nav, zoom, search, annotations, share
   - Mobile Compact App Bar: Back/Drawer toggle, Page Pill [ 2 / 7 ], Share, touch-native pinch-to-zoom
   - Floating AI Recommendation Pill / FAB: Frosted-glass pill floating above bottom nav (e.g. "✨ AI Suggestion: Redact PII (Contract) [Apply]") that collapses to a circular glowing FAB on scroll
   - Document Status Bar: PDF/A standard, byte size, encryption state, modification date
3. Right Dock (Desktop 260px) / Swipeable Bottom Sheet Drawer (Mobile): Contextual AI Tool Dock
   - Top: AI Recommendations block with confidence-ranked next best actions
   - On Mobile: Horizontal carousel of AI quick-action chips at the top of the bottom sheet
   - Body: Compact grouped tool rows (Signing, Security, Conversion, Page Formatting)
   - Bottom: Pinned Engine Badge ("🛡 Local Engine ACTIVE · 0 Cloud Bytes")
   - Full Apple HIG & Android Material 3 compliance: 48x48px touch targets on mobile
